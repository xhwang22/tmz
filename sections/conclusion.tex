\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

P2E treats evaluator evolution as learning how to acquire and interpret evidence
under a human evaluation standard. Structured preferences guide mechanism
hypotheses, while ERA explores implementations and selects complete programs with
a fixed base model. Across four domain families, the study design separates
alignment from critic-guided refinement and planned reward-guided training,
asking when better judgment can also improve generation.
