\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Learning an evaluator from human preferences requires specifying how evidence is
obtained and how quality judgments are combined. We formulate this learning
problem over executable evaluation programs and introduce \ERA{} as the revision
method. It connects human overall preferences to changes in
representation, criteria, specialist responsibilities, evidence acquisition,
routing, and synthesis while preserving pointwise deployment. The experimental
protocol separates adaptive development from terminal and held-out assessment,
and evaluates the scorer both as a judge and as a source of feedback.
Whether these revisions yield reliable gains remains to be established by the
planned controlled experiments.
