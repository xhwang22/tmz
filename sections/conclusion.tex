\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Human preferences must be turned into evaluation procedures that work on new outputs.
Disagreement-based preference acquisition targets redundant annotation; ERA separates candidate acceptance from direction continuation.
Matched-budget comparisons test whether these choices improve evaluation, while separate downstream tests assess selection, refinement, and reward-guided training.
The present placeholders and simulated illustrations provide no empirical evidence of these benefits.
