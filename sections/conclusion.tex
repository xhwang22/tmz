\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

P2E treats evaluator evolution as learning to acquire and interpret evidence
under a human evaluation standard. Reused and newly collected preferences
support the same learning problem, with separate provenance and quality audits.
ERA explores mechanism hypotheses and selects complete programs with a fixed
base model. Across four domain families, the study design separates alignment,
critic-guided refinement, and planned reward-guided training to ask when better
judgment improves generation. Simulated displays illustrate these comparisons
without resolving them.
