\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Open-ended generation requires evaluation systems that can acquire relevant
evidence and reconcile competing quality criteria. P2E connects semantic
disagreement mining and structured human feedback to executable evaluator
evolution. ERA supplies the revision method while keeping the scoring model
fixed. The research design covers four domain families and separates alignment
and selection, critic-guided refinement, and planned reward-guided training.
Independent measurements are still required to determine whether more informative
feedback and evidence-guided revisions improve evaluation and generation.
