\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

EvalOpt learns evaluation procedures from human preferences through disagreement-based preference mining and depth-first evaluator evolution.
It separates direction continuation from Train confirmation and global Val retention, allowing rejected candidates to inform further revisions without carrying their changes forward.
Held-out agreement, controlled search analyses, feedback efficiency, and independently judged downstream outputs test whether this approach uses limited human feedback effectively.
