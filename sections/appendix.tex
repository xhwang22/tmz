Representative resources include PPTAgent and PresentBench for presentations,
Design2Code for screenshot-conditioned webpages, SummEval for summarization,
and image-preference and alignment resources
\citep{pptagent2025,presentbench2026,si2025design2code,fabbri2021summeval,

Their outputs, references, metrics, and annotations have different evidential
roles; a resource is not automatically a source of the required human preference.
A benchmark collection spanning several standards remains a dataset collection,
not an additional domain. Research execution with a complete executable
postcondition is excluded, while ideation, study design, interpretation, writing,
and critique remain open-ended evaluation problems.

\paragraph{Dataset roles.}
Dev supports acquisition and evaluator development, ID tests unseen inputs from
the development source distribution, and OOD changes source distribution while
preserving the standard. External datasets provide separately locked assessment
only and do not determine semantic mappings or acquisition. Harder examples from
the same source are a difficulty stratum, not a new OOD domain. A domain selected
for confirmatory execution must pass the audit below; selection does not remove
unselected domains from the research scope.

\paragraph{Annotation units and levels.}
One annotation unit is an original input and its anonymized candidate group,
including permitted references. Pairs derived from that group remain dependent.
The core tuple in Equation~\ref{eq:human} is retained together with dataset and
license, source grouping, generator and prompt version, collection time, annotator
pool, agreement, and conversion provenance. Generator identity is recorded for
audit but hidden from annotators and the deployed scorer.

The planned evolution protocol collects $H_0$ and core $H_1$, with $H_2$ on
conflict and failure subsets. $H_3$ is optional. Two independent annotators with
adjudication may support development; final ID/OOD assessment targets three
independent annotators and blinded complete rankings. Core dimensions and the
tie policy are fixed in advance. A model or an author decision is not substituted
for a missing independent final annotation. Figures~\ref{fig:annotation}a--c
illustrate the distinctions without supplying study data.
An $H_0$-only instantiation must be reported as such; the framework's richer
interface does not establish that a run actually received $H_1$ or $H_2$.

\paragraph{Label-blind acquisition.}
Freeze domain knowledge, signal definitions, semantic mappings, and the
calibration recipe before accessing preference labels. Select a random anchor,
reveal only its authorized training judgments to estimate reliability, then
freeze the targeted sampling policy before opening labels in the remaining pool.
Charge anchor annotations to every strategy's human budget. An existing labeled
dataset must simulate the same access order. The final cohort is sampled
independently of conflicts and never contributes to reliability calibration.

\paragraph{Source and tool status.}
Registry entries distinguish executable, planned, and blocked sources
(Table~\ref{tab:readiness}).
A publication or code link without compatible inputs, weights, or dependencies
is not an executed signal. Available tools begin as candidates; a route must
explicitly activate them. Source independence is assessed at the signal level,
including model, training-label, and metric dependencies. Model-only comparisons
hold rubric, evidence, context, and reasoning budget fixed. Replacing both the
scoring model and its evidence-collection agent instead compares full systems.
Deterministic or fixed learned metrics are not duplicated for every judge model.

\section{Data access and independent assessment}
\label{app:boundaries}

Figure~\ref{fig:boundaries} separates proposal evidence, adaptive development
measurements, and terminal assessment. The scorer always receives the same
pointwise input contract, regardless of the cohort in which it is invoked.
Only the authorized diagnostic process sees human labels and pair identities.

The reserve cannot be reintroduced through tools, critique, reward, aliases,

\paragraph{Training and validation.}
Proposal-Train evidence may inform directions, revisions, and allocation.
Sealed Train reveals prespecified aggregate agreement, coverage, and stratum
guards without examples, labels, or per-case errors. Aggregates remain adaptive
development evidence, not a final performance estimate. The terminal shortlist
and its order are fixed from training evidence before Val access, and every
shortlisted outcome is reported. Repeated Val calls can assess stability but do
not create an independent test cohort. Interrupted searches do not obtain a new
terminal assessment merely because they stopped.

\paragraph{External metrics.}
The External Metric Reserve is frozen before search. Reserved metrics and their
equivalent wrappers are excluded from the executable tool library, router,
diagnostician, critique, reward, and checkpoint-selection criteria. If an endpoint
was used for optimization, it loses independent-assessment status and must be
replaced or explicitly reported as an optimization metric. This restriction
does not forbid using other existing metrics as tools.

\section{ERA algorithm and attribution contract}
\label{app:algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{ERA within the P2E framework}
\label{alg:era}
\small
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Seed $E_0$, proposal evidence $Z_{\mathrm{prop}}$, sealed development
interface, fixed budgets and measurement rule
\State Measure $E_0$ on the common sealed Train cohort; set best program $B\gets E_0$
\State Initialize direction history and allocation state
\While{search resources remain}
  \State Choose to request evidence, start, continue, pause, or switch direction
  \If{requesting evidence}
    \State Acquire authorized proposal-Train evidence; charge its cost
  \ElsIf{starting or continuing a direction}
    \State Set parent $P\gets B$ for a new direction, or the eligible working program
    \State Specify prediction, alternatives, target cases, and protection conditions
    \State Construct a bounded atomic edit or connected patch $E'\gets\operatorname{patch}(P)$
    \State Inspect local diagnostics; retain observations and failures
    \State Measure frozen $E'$ on the common sealed Train cohort
    \State Record the full attempt, component changes, costs, and aggregate outcome
    \If{measurement is complete and the development promotion rule holds}
      \State $B\gets E'$
    \EndIf
    \If{measurement is complete and the continuation rule holds}
      \State Retain $E'$ as the direction's working program
    \EndIf
    \State Update direction status without equating a failed candidate with refutation
  \Else
    \State Save the direction state and reallocate the remaining resources
  \EndIf
\EndWhile
\State On normal completion, freeze an eligible shortlist using training evidence only
\State Assess the shortlist on terminal Val under a prespecified selection rule
\State Freeze the final evaluator and analysis before independent Test/OOD assessment
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Hypothesis state.}
Each direction records a mechanism, its evidence, alternative explanations,
a falsifiable prediction, protected cases, editable components, and the
observations needed to assess the prediction. Local diagnostics are not a
mandatory success gate for broader sealed-development measurement.
Continuation retains a working program only when its measurement, new evidence,
and protection conditions justify doing so. The best program is a separate
selection state; retaining a working program does not redefine the best score.

\paragraph{Promotion and confirmation.}
Development promotion requires complete execution, a strict agreement gain,
nondecreasing coverage, and a prespecified worst-stratum regression guard.
Guard thresholds and tie rules are fixed before search. These are development
selection rules rather than statistical significance claims. Normal completion
is followed by fresh repeated terminal measurements of the frozen shortlist.
Selection uncertainty and the number of assessed candidates are reported.

\paragraph{Attribution.}
An elementary mutation changes one attributable component. A connected patch
may introduce a tool, a route, and a consumer change against one fixed parent.
The bundle receives one system-level outcome, not three independent causal
credits. Component attribution requires paired interventions with all other
interfaces held fixed, including tool on/off, evolved versus seed tool, and
adaptive versus static routing. If disabling a dependency makes a program
invalid, report that limitation and use a compatible control where justified.
System confirmation and attribution answer different questions.

A scoped refutation records the contradicted prediction and the evidence that
rules out relevant implementation alternatives. Missing observations, an
unselected tool, incorrect interpretation, or insufficient resources leave
different conclusions. Neither one unsuccessful implementation nor a
nonsignificant difference alone refutes the direction.

\section{Semantic synthesis and anchored scoring}
\label{app:synthesis}

Semantic synthesis is an optional fusion procedure, compared with a declared
numerical rule. A specialist report contains a finding identifier, supporting
evidence, a dimension, uncertainty, and an anchored quality judgment.
The synthesizer groups findings into factors while preserving overlap and
contradiction (Figure~\ref{fig:synthesis}a--b). Duplicate descriptions of the same
defect should not become independent penalties, and a favorable style judgment
should not silently cancel a factual error when the domain standard prioritizes
faithfulness.

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\input{figures/semantic_synthesis}
\caption{Optional semantic synthesis. Panel (a) supplies sourced findings;
(b) groups overlapping and conflicting statements into factors; (c) locates
overall quality between prespecified anchors. Every finding reference is assigned
once, but bookkeeping alone cannot validate the grouping or the quality judgment.
The anchor values and interpolation are a synthetic arithmetic example, not an
evaluator result or a universal scale for all domains.}
\label{fig:synthesis}
\end{figure}

Let $a_k<a_{k+1}$ be adjacent declared anchors and $\lambda\in[0,1]$ locate
quality between them. The resulting scalar is
\begin{equation}
 s=(1-\lambda)a_k+\lambda a_{k+1}.
 \label{eq:anchors}
\end{equation}
The anchors have domain-specific verbal meanings. Finding-level scores with
different meanings are not averaged merely because they are numeric.
Reference completeness, semantic correctness, and calibration are separate
properties and require separate checks. Figure~\ref{fig:synthesis}c illustrates
only the arithmetic, using $a_k=50$, $a_{k+1}=75$, and $\lambda=0.4$.

\section{Measurement and statistical analysis}
\label{app:measurement}

\paragraph{Completeness and denominators.}
For a prespecified strict-preference cohort of $N$ pairs, let $c_i=1$ when both
artifacts have all $R$ valid complete scores. Let
$m=\sum_i c_i$ and $k=\sum_{i:c_i=1}z_i$, with $z_i$ from
Equation~\ref{eq:measurement}. Report completed-pair accuracy $k/m$ when $m>0$,
coverage $m/N$, and the missing-outcome interval
\begin{equation}
 \left[\frac{k}{N},\frac{k+N-m}{N}\right].
 \label{eq:missing}
\end{equation}
This interval bounds full-cohort agreement under arbitrary missing outcomes;
it is not a statistical confidence interval. A missing repeat is not replaced
by the median of surviving calls. Completeness includes selected routes and
tools, not only a final score. A valid observation that an artifact fails a
quality check differs from an execution error.

\paragraph{Ranking and selection endpoints.}
For the default $N=4$ design, human complete rankings support all strict pairs.
Global agreement averages within input groups before aggregation across inputs.
Top-region agreement uses prespecified strict comparisons involving at least
one human top-two candidate. Best-of-$N$ measures selection of a human top-ranked
candidate; normalized rank regret is defined in Section~\ref{sec:core}.
Human ties, evaluator ties, and incomplete rankings are governed by a frozen
policy and reported separately. Pair-only data do not supply missing ranks or
cardinal utility. Core $H_1$ agreement is reported by dimension before any macro
average.

\paragraph{Randomness and repeated use.}
Freeze $R\geq3$, score precision, retry policy, artifact reuse, and cache identity.
Retries are outcome-blind and uniformly applied. Repeated scores are not
independent task observations. Paired uncertainty uses original-input/source
group resampling, preserving candidate groups and comparisons; the planned
interval level is 95\%. Report independent search seeds separately from scoring
repeats, and distinguish within-run execution variation from between-search
variation. Retain failed, rejected, incomplete, and interrupted attempts in the
authorized analysis, without favorable resampling or denominator changes.

\paragraph{C2/C3 human outcomes.}
Report wins, losses, ties, invalid outputs, and major regressions per input group.
A prespecified tie-aware preference probability may assign half credit to a tie,
with its denominator and missingness bounds stated. Report C2 changes against
the shared $y_0$ in addition to the critic contrast, and C3 changes against Base
in addition to the reward contrast. Intervals and costs accompany effect sizes.
Multiple domains, dimensions, and ablations require declared primary contrasts
and a prespecified multiplicity policy. A search-selected development maximum
is not substituted for any final endpoint.

and critic usefulness. It is not an executed experiment, and no parameter

Under the controls in Table~\ref{tab:training}, the frozen reward produces scalar
scores within each rollout group using the same normalization and advantage rule.
Zero-variance groups, invalid artifacts,
and execution failures require a common prespecified policy. Deterministic
checks may enforce format or executability, but do not define the entire reward
for open-ended quality.

The pilot uses development validation only. If its prespecified stability or
reward-hacking gate fails, expansion stops for that domain and the failure is
retained. Success elsewhere is not a substitute. Final sample sizes are determined
from pilot variance and a declared minimum detectable effect before test
generation. Test inputs and judgments do not participate in training, checkpoint
selection, reward revision, or failure-driven edits. Any deliberate sharing of
locked input pools across C1/C2/C3 must be prespecified with an access audit;
their newly generated outputs and judgments are never treated as reusable old
labels.
