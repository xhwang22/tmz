\section{Scope and problem formulation}
\label{sec:problem}

A \emph{domain} is a collection of tasks sharing core quality dimensions, their
priorities, the overall decision rule, and the human annotation protocol.
An artifact type does not define a domain: screenshot-conditioned webpage
reconstruction and specification-conditioned webpage generation require
different standards. A change of source distribution that preserves the standard
is instead a within-domain distribution shift. Appendix~\ref{app:domains}
enumerates the full research scope of 22 domains in four families, without
assuming that all are ready for experiments. Closed tasks whose complete quality
criterion is an executable postcondition fall outside this scope.

Let $x$ be a task with permitted context, $y$ a candidate output, and
$\mathcal{Q}_D=\{q_1,\ldots,q_m\}$ the quality dimensions for domain $D$.
A human comparison has the core form
\begin{equation}
 h=(x,y_a,y_b,q,p,a,\nu),
 \label{eq:human}
\end{equation}
where $q$ is a dimension or overall quality, $p$ is a preference or tie,
$a$ records annotator agreement, and $\nu$ records provenance.
Feedback has four levels: $H_0$ gives overall preference; $H_1$ gives
dimension-wise preference; $H_2$ identifies error type, severity, and location;
$H_3$ optionally supplies a free-form rationale. $H_1$ is central to the
dimension-guided design. An $H_0$-only corpus remains usable, but it supplies
neither human dimensional judgments nor human explanations.

The learned object is an executable evaluation program
\begin{equation}
 E_\theta=(A,K,V,T,\Pi,G),\qquad (s_\theta,\tau_\theta)=E_\theta(x,y),
 \label{eq:program}
\end{equation}
comprising artifact adapters $A$, dimension schema and rubrics $K$, evaluator
modules $V$, tools $T$, evidence routing $\Pi$, and fusion $G$.
The score $s_\theta$ is accompanied by an execution trace $\tau_\theta$.
The base model is fixed outside $\theta$.
At deployment, the evaluator receives one candidate and its permitted evidence;
opponents, human labels, generator identity, split labels, and mining strata
are excluded. We seek agreement with unseen human preferences at an explicitly
reported evaluation cost, rather than a single judge shared across all domains.
