\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

ERA makes the program's approximation of human evaluation standards editable
through feedback. Explicit criteria and evidence paths permit inspection, but
whether they faithfully explain judgments requires intervention tests.
Comparison with parameter adaptation asks whether evolving this structure is
useful; not all disagreement is a structural failure.

\subsection{Limitations and future work}

First, simulated displays support no effectiveness claim and must be replaced
with frozen empirical cohorts. Second, dimensional judgments require additional
annotation and construct validation. Third, shared signal bias and interacting
components require dependence audits and controlled attribution. Finally,
pointwise scoring assumes a useful within-domain ordering, while repeated
development can overfit aggregate feedback. Independent ID/OOD and generation-level
studies must test evaluation gains and downstream utility; transfer across
standards remains a separate question.
