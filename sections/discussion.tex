\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Interpreting improvement.}
Mining and evolution are assessed by the final evaluator obtained per annotation and search cost.
Diagnostic and controller contrasts test the value of execution evidence, target normalization, and adaptive edit budgets.
Longer directions or favorable individual trajectories cannot substitute for these comparisons.

\paragraph{Scope and limitations.}
Separately adapted, within-domain evaluators do not establish universal transfer.
Trace audits distinguish observed behavior from the agent's account of its correction.
Agreement also need not improve downstream quality, and reward optimization can amplify evaluator errors \citep{wang2026rewardhacking}.
Independent downstream judgments therefore remain separate endpoints.
