\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What would support deeper search?}
Later gains over history-matched search and better held-out selection would support retaining directions independently of candidate acceptance.
Longer directions alone are insufficient; the parent-reset control separates direction priority from retaining intermediate implementations.

\paragraph{Scope and limitations.}
Separately adapted, within-domain evaluators do not establish universal transfer.
The controller retains direction identifiers and diagnostics but cannot verify semantic continuity or sufficient reasons to end; trace audits must separate observations from agent interpretation.
Agreement also need not improve downstream quality, and reward optimization can amplify evaluator errors \citep{wang2026rewardhacking}.
Fresh human judgments and reserved attribute checks remain necessary; the scenario establishes no empirical benefit.
