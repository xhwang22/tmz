\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Retaining evidence beyond a candidate.}
EvalOpt separates the lifetime of an implementation from that of the evidence it produces.
Rollback discards provisional changes but preserves diagnostics.
Depth-first evolution uses this evidence to decide whether further work is warranted and what to test next, rather than prescribing more attempts.

\paragraph{Scope and limitations.}
Diagnostic continuation relies on records that help assess why a revision failed.
Aggregate outcomes alone may not justify further development; reduced feedback tests the additional value of case-level traces.
The evolution agent's interpretations can be wrong, motivating independent trajectory review.
We do not evaluate cross-domain transfer of evolved evaluators.
Higher evaluator--human agreement need not improve downstream outputs, especially when reward optimization amplifies residual evaluator errors \citep{wang2026rewardhacking}.
C2 and C3 therefore assess outputs through independent blinded judgments, not the evolved evaluator.
