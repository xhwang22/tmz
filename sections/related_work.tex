\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Learning and assessing evaluators.}
G-Eval and MT-Bench study model-based judgments and their relationship to human
evaluation \citep{liu2023geval,zheng2023judging}. Prometheus and Prometheus~2 train
models for rubric-conditioned evaluation, while Self-Taught Evaluators use
synthetic supervision to improve judges
\citep{kim2024prometheus,kim2024prometheus2,wang2024selftaught}.
EvalGen emphasizes that evaluation criteria themselves require alignment with
human preferences \citep{shankar2024evalgen}. RewardBench and JudgeBench test
evaluators on challenging comparisons, including cases where preference and
objective correctness require careful distinction
\citep{lambert2024rewardbench,tan2025judgebench}.
\ERA{} instead holds model weights fixed and revises the executable evaluation
procedure. Overall human preference is its supervision target; inferred
explanations are not additional human annotations.

\paragraph{Optimizing language-model programs.}
DSPy exposes compositional language-model pipelines to optimization
\citep{khattab2024dspy}. ProTeGi, OPRO, TextGrad, and GEPA use model-generated
feedback or proposals to improve instructions and programs
\citep{pryzant2023protegi,yang2024opro,yuksekgonul2025textgrad,agrawal2026gepa}.
ADAS, AFlow, and the Darwin G\"odel Machine further investigate the automated
construction or revision of agentic systems
\citep{hu2025adas,zhang2025aflow,zhang2025dgm}.
Their broad search spaces make them relevant baselines for assessing an
evaluator-specific representation and evidence interface. \ERA{} specializes
these interfaces to evaluation, where a ranking failure must be related to
observations, criteria, and synthesis.

\paragraph{Artifact quality and downstream use.}
Evaluation standards differ across domains. SummEval and BERTScore address
summarization evaluation and text similarity
\citep{fabbri2021summeval,zhang2020bertscore}; ImageReward, Pick-a-Pic, and GenEval
capture different aspects of image preference and text--image alignment
\citep{xu2023imagereward,kirstain2023pickapic,ghosh2023geneval}.
Such signals may supply evidence or comparisons, but are not interchangeable
definitions of overall quality. ReAct, Reflexion, and Self-Refine show ways to
combine observations, reflection, and revision
\citep{yao2023react,shinn2023reflexion,madaan2023selfrefine}.
Preference-based summarization, instruction tuning, and direct preference
optimization connect evaluation to model learning
\citep{stiennon2020summarize,ouyang2022instruct,rafailov2023dpo}.
The proposed evaluation focuses on an evolved evaluator as a selector and critic;
reward-guided parameter training is outside the empirical scope.
