\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Evaluators and feedback acquisition.}
G-Eval and MT-Bench study model-based judgment and human agreement
\citep{liu2023geval,zheng2023judging}. Prometheus, Prometheus~2, and Self-Taught
Evaluators improve judges through rubric-conditioned or synthetic supervision
\citep{kim2024prometheus,kim2024prometheus2,wang2024selftaught}.
EvalGen emphasizes aligning the criteria themselves with human preferences,
while RewardBench and JudgeBench assess evaluators on challenging comparisons
\citep{shankar2024evalgen,lambert2024rewardbench,tan2025judgebench}.
Disagreement-based acquisition has a long history in query-by-committee
\citep{seung1992committee}. P2E applies this principle to an ecosystem whose
signals need not measure the same construct. Its semantic mapping distinguishes
same-dimension conflict from trade-offs and records source dependence.
Dimension-wise human judgments can then locate discrepancies that an overall
label alone does not resolve.

\paragraph{Optimizing language-model programs.}
DSPy exposes compositional pipelines to optimization \citep{khattab2024dspy}.
ProTeGi, OPRO, TextGrad, and GEPA use model-generated feedback or proposals to
improve instructions and programs
\citep{pryzant2023protegi,yang2024opro,yuksekgonul2025textgrad,agrawal2026gepa}.
ADAS, AFlow, and the Darwin G\"odel Machine investigate automated construction
or evolution of agentic systems \citep{hu2025adas,zhang2025aflow,zhang2025dgm}.
Their search spaces provide relevant controls, rather than a basis for assuming
that prior methods cannot change structure. ERA specializes the representation
and evidence interface to evaluation, linking ranking failures to observations,
criteria, and synthesis while separating an implementation outcome from its
underlying direction hypothesis.

\paragraph{Domain quality and downstream use.}
SummEval and BERTScore address summarization evaluation and text similarity
\citep{fabbri2021summeval,zhang2020bertscore}; ImageReward, Pick-a-Pic, and GenEval
capture different aspects of image preference and alignment
\citep{xu2023imagereward,kirstain2023pickapic,ghosh2023geneval}.
These signals are useful evidence, not interchangeable definitions of quality.
ReAct, Reflexion, and Self-Refine connect observations, reflection, and revision
\citep{yao2023react,shinn2023reflexion,madaan2023selfrefine}.
Human-feedback summarization, instruction tuning, and direct preference
optimization connect evaluation to model learning
\citep{stiennon2020summarize,ouyang2022instruct,rafailov2023dpo}.
P2E therefore separates fixed-pool evaluation, critic-guided refinement, and
reward-guided training as distinct tests of an evolved evaluator.
