\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Evaluators and data mining.}
G-Eval and MT-Bench study model-based judgment and human agreement \citep{liu2023geval,zheng2023judging}.
Prometheus, Prometheus~2, and Self-Taught Evaluators improve judges through rubric-conditioned or synthetic training data \citep{kim2024prometheus,kim2024prometheus2,wang2024selftaught}.
AutoCalibrate refines evaluation criteria from human-labeled examples, while EvalGen helps users define criteria and select evaluation functions using human grades \citep{liu2024autocalibrate,shankar2024evalgen}.
For DeepResearch reports, \citet{lv2026queryrubrics} train query-specific rubric generators from human preferences and use the learned rubrics as training rewards.
Our optimization target includes both the criteria and the procedure for gathering and using evaluation evidence.

RewardBench and JudgeBench assess evaluators on preference comparisons \citep{lambert2024rewardbench,tan2025judgebench}.
Benchmark$^2$ instead examines benchmark quality through ranking consistency, discriminability, and capability alignment, and studies selective test-set construction \citep{qian2026benchmark2}.
Our data-mining question concerns which comparisons to select for evaluator improvement, reusing matching preferences or collecting missing ones, drawing on query-by-committee and active preference learning \citep{seung1992committee,muldrew2024activepreference}.
Because our evaluation signals can measure different properties, IPM distinguishes conflicts within a dimension from trade-offs across dimensions and retains random sampling to check shared blind spots.

\paragraph{Optimizing language-model programs.}
DSPy exposes compositional pipelines to optimization \citep{khattab2024dspy}.
ProTeGi, OPRO, TextGrad, and GEPA use model-generated feedback or proposals to improve instructions and programs \citep{pryzant2023protegi,yang2024opro,yuksekgonul2025textgrad,agrawal2026gepa}.
ADAS, AFlow, and the Darwin G\"odel Machine investigate automated construction or evolution of agentic systems \citep{hu2025adas,zhang2025aflow,zhang2025dgm}.
GEPA retains candidates with complementary strengths, while DGM explores an archive of agent implementations.
Studying the path from experience generation through skill extraction to consumption, \citet{huang2026skillconsumption} report average gains alongside negative transfer and differences between a model's ability to extract and use skills.
Their study tests whether extracted skills help a target agent; ERA examines how to continue an evaluator-improvement direction after an unsuccessful implementation, separately from accepting that candidate.

\paragraph{Domain quality and downstream use.}
SummEval and BERTScore address summarization evaluation and text similarity \citep{fabbri2021summeval,zhang2020bertscore}; ImageReward, Pick-a-Pic, and GenEval capture different aspects of image preference and alignment \citep{xu2023imagereward,kirstain2023pickapic,ghosh2023geneval}.
These signals are useful evidence, not interchangeable definitions of quality.
ReAct, Reflexion, and Self-Refine connect observations, reflection, and iterative improvement \citep{yao2023react,shinn2023reflexion,madaan2023selfrefine}.
Human-feedback summarization, instruction tuning, and direct preference optimization connect evaluation to model learning \citep{stiennon2020summarize,ouyang2022instruct,rafailov2023dpo}.
The reward-hacking survey of \citet{wang2026rewardhacking} organizes risks of proxy optimization around objective compression, optimization amplification, and evaluator--policy co-adaptation.
An evaluator's agreement on fixed outputs therefore needs to be distinguished from its behavior under optimization, motivating separate tests of fixed-pool selection, critic-guided refinement, and reward-guided training.
