\section*{AI use statement}

Generative AI assisted manuscript drafting, literature organization, and the
preparation of editable LaTeX conceptual diagrams and plotting code.
Figure~\ref{fig:teaser} uses AI-generated illustrative images from an earlier figure-design pass, with editable vector labels and search paths.
Its preferences and intermediate outcomes are constructed, not observed annotations or optimization traces.
The quantitative displays use explicitly simulated
data, generated offline for reviewing the reporting design. They are not observed
cases, human annotations, model executions, or performance measurements. No
empirical results or parameter-training runs were produced for this draft.
Bibliographic identities were checked against primary metadata
sources. Author review, responsibility for the final content, and the final
disclosure of research-stage AI use remain to be completed before submission.

\section*{Ethics statement}

Human preferences can encode annotator and model biases rather than a universal
definition of quality. Domain-specific criteria and uncertainty should remain
visible, especially for automated research judgments. Any new annotation study
must document consent, compensation, privacy protection, and the applicable
institutional review determination before data collection. Artifact licenses
and source permissions must be checked independently of technical accessibility.
Generated code and evidence tools require restricted execution and controlled
external access. Reward-guided optimization requires checks for exploitation of
the evaluator and degradation outside its favored criteria. No private artifacts,
identifiable annotation records, or experimental traces are included in this
working manuscript. Study-specific approvals and release decisions are pending.
