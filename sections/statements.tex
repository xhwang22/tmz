\section*{Ethics statement}

Human preferences can encode annotator and model biases rather than a universal
definition of quality. Domain-specific criteria and uncertainty should remain
visible, especially for automated research judgments. Any new annotation study
must document consent, compensation, privacy protection, and the applicable
institutional review determination before data collection. Artifact licenses
and source permissions must be checked independently of technical accessibility.
Generated code and evidence tools require restricted execution and controlled
external access. Reward-guided optimization requires checks for exploitation of
the evaluator and degradation outside its favored criteria. No private artifacts,
identifiable annotation records, or experimental traces are included in this
working manuscript. Study-specific approvals and release decisions are pending.
