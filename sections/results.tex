The Seed comparison measures improvement over the starting evaluator; learned signal fusion tests whether reweighting fixed signals suffices without revising the procedure.
Optimizer comparisons test search effectiveness with the same feedback and budget (Table~\ref{tab:main-alignment}).
All methods score the same outputs independently, without access to human labels or competing outputs.
