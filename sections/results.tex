\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Tables~\ref{tab:results} and \ref{tab:downstream-results} reserve the core
reporting structure. Numerical results have not been incorporated into this
manuscript. Every dash denotes an unmeasured quantity, not zero, and no method
ordering is implied. Estimates will be reported per domain and cohort before
any cross-domain summary. Appendix~\ref{app:results} specifies additional
dimensional, reliability, cost, and ablation outputs.
