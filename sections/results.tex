\section{Quantitative evaluation: simulated displays}
\label{sec:results}

All numerical displays in this section use \emph{simulated data}, not measured evaluator performance or collected human judgments.
They instantiate the reporting protocol (Appendix~\ref{app:simulation}); their assigned outcomes provide no evidence for either research hypothesis.

\paragraph{Alignment across evaluation standards.}
Average agreement can conceal opposing effects across tasks.
Figure~\ref{fig:outcomes}c retains all 22 domains in four families, comparing five evaluator fixtures on OOD inputs.
Panel (a) reports their balanced input composition, while panel (b) relates agreement to assigned evaluation cost without pooling the two feedback settings.
Task order follows the taxonomy, not performance.
The two metric-only references remain in Table~\ref{tab:results}; Figure~\ref{fig:alignment} reports paired ERA-minus-seed effects on the full ID/OOD cohorts.

\paragraph{What changes in the evaluator, and what feedback supports it?}
Figure~\ref{fig:ablation}a reports six conditional full-minus-fixed component contrasts in eight diagnostic tasks, not additive contributions.
Panel (b) varies cumulative feedback detail on the same input groups.
Granularity comparisons must also account for annotation time and unequal label availability; they do not test acquisition efficiency.

\paragraph{Feedback utility, acquisition, and resources.}
Alignment does not establish useful critique.
Figure~\ref{fig:refinement}a--b retains C2 quality distributions and unresolved judgments for the eight diagnostic tasks, on inputs distinct from C1.
Both feedback settings require fresh blinded endpoint judgments for C2.
Figure~\ref{fig:acquisition}a--b isolates acquisition policy at equal human budgets, including a common random anchor.
Figure~\ref{fig:annotation-quality}a--c keeps agreement, abstention, and consensus denominators distinct; label origin does not establish reliability.
Selection profiles (Figure~\ref{fig:selection}) and deployment costs (Figure~\ref{fig:cost}) retain separate denominators.
C3 remains entirely unmeasured.
