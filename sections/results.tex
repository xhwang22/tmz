\paragraph{C2: critic-guided refinement.}
A frozen generator refines the same fresh $y_0$ using Seed or EvalOpt critiques, or no critique \citep{madaan2023selfrefine}, at matched resources.
Figure~\ref{fig:downstream}a reports the EvalOpt-versus-Seed contrast separately for each representative domain.
The no-critic comparison tests the value of critique beyond another revision, while $y_0$ anchors nondegradation (Table~\ref{tab:downstream-detail}).
Both require new blinded judgments of the exact outputs, independent of evaluator scores.

\paragraph{C3: reward-guided training.}
Figure~\ref{fig:downstream}b compares generators trained with frozen Seed and EvalOpt rewards, using the same starting checkpoint and policy-training recipe within each domain \citep{hu2022lora,shao2024deepseekmath}.
New human judgments assess the trained outputs; Base provides the no-update reference.
Reward preflight precedes training, with failed or incomplete studies reported under the fixed roster (Appendix~\ref{app:training}).
