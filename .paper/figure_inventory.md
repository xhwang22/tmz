CSV paths are relative to data/simulated/. Native 5.4-inch vector charts are
generated by scripts/render_simulated_results.py. The five method colors are
blue, mint (static-tools seed), cream, peach, and rose (ERA). Other plots have
explicit family, H-level, policy, or outcome mappings. Color does not replace
numeric scales, task labels, setting tags, or uncertainty.

C1 covers the complete 22-domain taxonomy in synthetic form. The original eight
C1 group records and diagnostic data are retained; fourteen C1 domains are
added solely for layout review. Two construction settings are illustrative,
not label-readiness claims. C2/annotation/component detail remains an explicitly
bounded subset and cannot support full-domain empirical claims.
