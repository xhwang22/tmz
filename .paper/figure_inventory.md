The ocean/sea-glass/sand/coral palette follows the three author references.
Charts use TeX Gyre Heros; diagrams use scoped Helvetica-compatible text. Plot
titles, axes, legends, and notes share an explicit size hierarchy. Method
comparisons retain coral for ERA and blue for the static tool-augmented seed.
Rank colors, settings, and signed effects are separately defined in captions.
See `visual_references.md` for the design rationale and inspected source papers.

`make simulated` regenerates the fixtures and hash manifest offline. `make figures`
produces isolated vector PDFs, PNGs, and a contact sheet under
`build/figure-review/`; it makes no model calls. Inspect both standalone plots
and final manuscript pages at print size. Do not silently rescale unreadable
labels to fit a page.

The author authorized these simulated layout examples, not fabricated research
findings. Every CSV row, chart, and caption carries a disclosure. Real plots
require authorized artifacts, explicit units and denominators, matched controls,
appropriate uncertainty, and a fresh claim audit. The submission gate must fail
while fixtures remain.
